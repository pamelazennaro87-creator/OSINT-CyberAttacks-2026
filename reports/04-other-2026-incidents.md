# Other Notable 2026 Incidents

## Star Aviation (Kentucky) – Storm Ransomware Claim

- **Date**: September 2026
- **Target**: Aerospace supplier specializing in engine wire harnesses and electronic interconnect systems for Boeing and Airbus aircraft
- **Claim**: Storm ransomware group
- **Exposed samples**: Technical schematics and possible employee ID cards
- **Risk**: Potential aid to fraud, social engineering or future attacks against aviation supply chain

## CyberLogitec / Maritime Cascade

- **Date**: Claims published ~12 October 2026, deadline 19 October
- **Pattern**: Possible single breach of South Korean maritime/port software provider (CyberLogitec) leading to claims against 38 companies across 18 countries
- **Ransom demands**: Aggregate claims up to ~$1.22 billion (highly inflated typical of leak-site pressure)
- **Notable claimed victims**: Ocean Network Express (ONE), various terminal operators, ZPMC, NEOM Ports, etc.
- **Analysis**: Classic supply-chain cascade – one compromised software vendor used to pressure multiple downstream operators

## Korean Air & Asiana Airlines via Amadeus

- **Window**: August 2023 – September 2026 (long-running)
- **Vector**: Valid login credentials to Amadeus reservation/ticketing system accounts
- **Data**: Booking reference numbers exposed; payment data not affected
- **Notification**: Airlines notified by Amadeus mid-October 2026
- **Lesson**: Third-party reservation platform credential hygiene remains a persistent risk

## Namibia Airports Company – INC Ransomware

- **Date**: March 2026
- **Claim**: ~500 GB (financial, HR, customer data)
- **Status**: Claimed but no public release confirmed at time of earlier reporting

## Notes

These incidents reinforce the 2026 pattern of targeting vendors and ancillary systems rather than (publicly confirmed) core flight operations systems.
